A quick tour

1
Log your day

On Today, press the big play button when you start working and stop when you finish, and the time is added for you. Prefer typing? Tap the pencil and enter hours like 7h 45m or time slots like 8:00-12:00, 13:00-15:00. PTO and sick leave can share a day with worked time.

2
Pause for breaks

Pause the timer for a break and press play to carry on. Your hiker walks, snacks and sleeps along with you.

3
Watch your balance

Your balance is the plus or minus against your target. The weather shows how close you are to the monthly limit, and the trail shows the quarter, where you should be back at zero. Past working days with nothing logged count as 0h until you fill them in.

4
Collect stars

Every week is a constellation with one star per working day. Log a day and its star lights up. The Stars page shows your whole year.

5
Calendar, leave and holidays

Click any day in the Calendar to edit it. Click the PTO or Sick bottle on Today to book dates. Pick your country in Settings to get public holidays, and export a backup there every so often.
